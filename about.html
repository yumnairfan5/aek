Advocate, Telangana High Court

Ahmed Ehtesham
Kawkab

Ahmed Ehtesham Kawkab has been practising at the Telangana Bar since 2007. Enrolled with the Bar Council of Telangana, he has spent nearly two decades building a practice centred on rigorous legal reasoning, procedural discipline, and a genuine commitment to the interests of those he represents.

His work spans criminal defence, civil litigation, constitutional writs, corporate insolvency, cyber law, family disputes, service matters, and FIR quashing. He practises regularly before the Telangana High Court, NCLT Hyderabad, and courts across India, including High Courts, civil courts, and criminal courts.

He approaches every matter with the same standard of preparation regardless of its size. His clients range from individuals facing criminal proceedings for the first time to companies navigating complex insolvency processes. What holds across all of it is a straightforward principle: understand the facts completely, know the law thoroughly, and advance the client's position without compromise.

The Record

19 Years at the Bar.

Nineteen years at the Telangana Bar have produced a practice grounded in experience rather than assumption. From bail hearings before sessions courts to writ petitions before the High Court, the range of matters handled reflects both breadth and depth of exposure to Indian litigation.

His approach to procedural strategy is deliberate: each matter is assessed on its own facts, the correct forum is identified early, and a clear plan is communicated to the client before proceedings begin. Long-term outcomes are given priority over short-term convenience.
