Criminal Law

Criminal proceedings move quickly and the consequences of poor representation are irreversible. Ahmed Ehtesham Kawkab has handled criminal matters across bail, trial, and appeal stages before sessions courts, the Telangana High Court, and special tribunals. His approach is built on a thorough reading of the case diary, early identification of procedural irregularities, and a clear-eyed assessment of the evidence.

He represents individuals in a wide range of offences including IPC matters, PMLA cases, NDPS, cheque dishonour, and cyber-linked criminal complaints. Every matter is handled with the same standard of preparation regardless of the complexity of the charge.

Matters Handled

Applications for anticipatory bail and regular bail hearings across all sessions and High Court benches.
Petitions to quash First Information Reports and criminal proceedings where continuation would be an abuse of process. Covers both compoundable and non-compoundable offences.
Full trial representation from Sessions Court through High Court appeals and revisions. Strategic examination, cross-examination, and final arguments in complex criminal trials.
Defence in Prevention of Money Laundering Act matters, cheque dishonour, and complex economic fraud allegations before Special Courts and High Courts.

02

Civil Litigation

Property disputes, contractual claims, and recovery suits form the core of civil litigation practice. Ahmed Ehtesham Kawkab approaches civil matters with a focus on early strategic assessment: what the client's strongest claim is, what interim relief is available, and what a realistic outcome looks like at trial. This clarity at the outset saves time and prevents proceedings from dragging on without direction.

His civil practice spans the City Civil Courts, Jr. Civil Courts in Secunderabad, and Ranga Reddy District Court, with appellate matters taken to the Telangana High Court. He handles injunction applications, partition suits, specific performance, debt recovery, and execution proceedings.

Matters Handled

Suits for specific performance of agreements for sale, breach of contract, and damages. Includes injunction applications and interim relief in property and commercial disputes.
Partition suits, declaration of title, and recovery of possession across urban and agricultural property. Title searches, mutation disputes, and encumbrance resolution.
Order XXXVII CPC summary suits, recovery of money, and Debt Recovery Tribunal representation for financial institutions and individual creditors.
Temporary and permanent injunctions, stay applications, and execution of decrees. Includes attachment before judgment and arrest of judgment debtors.

Cases on Record

03

Writ Jurisdiction

When a government authority exceeds its powers, acts in violation of natural justice, or infringes on fundamental rights, the appropriate remedy lies in the writ jurisdiction of the High Court. Ahmed Ehtesham Kawkab files and argues writ petitions before the Telangana High Court in matters involving arbitrary state action, unlawful detention, wrongful denial of entitlements, and constitutional challenges to administrative orders.

His experience with the standard of judicial review applied to government orders allows him to identify which writs are maintainable, what interim stay is realistic, and how best to frame the grounds for challenge. He also handles counter-affidavits and representation on behalf of respondents in writ proceedings.

Matters Handled

Petitions for release from unlawful detention and writs compelling public authorities to perform statutory duties. Urgent hearing applications in habeas corpus matters.
Challenging quasi-judicial orders passed without jurisdiction, in violation of natural justice, or against the weight of evidence. Prohibition against tribunals exceeding jurisdiction.
PIL filings on environmental, civic, and constitutional issues affecting the public. Drafting affidavits, counter-affidavits, and representation before Division Benches.
Writ petitions challenging government orders on appointments, dismissals, regularisation, and policy decisions affecting individual rights under statutory service rules.

Cases on Record

Writ Municipality & Urban Development

Writ Revenue Records Rectification

Chakali Sattaiah vs. The State of Telangana Writ Petition (Civil) for rectification of entries in revenue records. Before Hon'ble K. Lakshman, Telangana High Court, Single Bench. Disposed.

04

Cyber Law

Cyber crime cases present unique evidentiary challenges. Electronic records, digital forensics, and the admissibility of data obtained from devices or online platforms require careful legal analysis that goes beyond standard criminal procedure. Ahmed Ehtesham Kawkab has handled matters involving online fraud, phishing, hacking, unauthorised access, and digital impersonation.

He advises individuals and businesses on their rights following data breaches and privacy violations, and represents accused persons in bail, trial, and appellate proceedings before Cyber Crime Courts and the High Court. He also advises platforms and intermediaries on their compliance obligations under the IT Rules.

Matters Handled

Defence in cases including hacking, phishing, online fraud, and unauthorized access. Bail and trial representation before Cyber Crime Courts.
Adjudication proceedings before the IT Adjudicating Officer for data breaches, privacy violations, and disputes.
Advising platforms and intermediaries on safe harbour protections, take-down notices, and due diligence obligations.
Admissibility of electronic records, expert witness coordination, and challenging forensic evidence in criminal and civil proceedings.

Corporate & NCLT

Corporate disputes and insolvency proceedings demand a practitioner who understands both the procedural requirements of the tribunal and the commercial stakes involved. Ahmed Ehtesham Kawkab represents financial creditors, operational creditors, resolution applicants, and respondent companies in proceedings before the National Company Law Tribunal.

His corporate practice extends to shareholder disputes, oppression and mismanagement petitions, domestic arbitration, and advisory on company law compliance. He assists companies and individuals navigating the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, from CIRP initiation through resolution or liquidation.

Matters Handled

CIRP applications by financial and operational creditors before NCLT Hyderabad. Representation of resolution applicants, committee of creditors, and resolution professionals.
Representation in company law matters before the NCLT, including disputes between stakeholders, creditor actions, and proceedings under applicable statutory frameworks.
Representation in commercial disputes arising from contracts, including recovery actions, breach claims, and related proceedings before civil courts and the High Court.
Assistance in company-related disputes, including shareholder disagreements, contractual issues, and proceedings arising under company law before appropriate forums.

Family Law

Family disputes are among the most personally consequential matters a person can face. Ahmed Ehtesham Kawkab handles matrimonial and family cases with the seriousness they require: a careful understanding of the client's position, an honest assessment of what the law permits, and a strategy calibrated to the specific forum and applicable personal law statute.

He represents clients in divorce proceedings, custody and guardianship disputes, maintenance applications, domestic violence complaints, and succession matters. He practises across Family Courts in Hyderabad and the Telangana High Court, and advises on negotiated settlements where that is in the client's interest.

Matters Handled

Contested and mutual consent divorce proceedings across personal law statutes.
Custody disputes, visitation arrangements, and Guardianship and Wards Act petitions. International child abduction matters and Hague Convention applications.
Proceedings under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act. Protection orders, residence orders, and compensation applications before Magistrate Courts.
Testamentary and intestate succession matters. Probate applications, letters of administration, succession certificates, and disputed will proceedings.

Service Law

Government employees facing disciplinary action, withheld promotions, pension disputes, or arbitrary transfers often have strong legal remedies available to them, but only if pursued in the right forum with the right framing.

Ahmed Ehtesham Kawkab represents employees in service-related disputes, including disciplinary proceedings, promotion issues, pension matters, and challenges to administrative decisions before appropriate forums and the High Court.

Matters Handled

Original Applications before Central Administrative Tribunal and State Administrative Tribunal. Transfer petitions and writ petitions challenging tribunal orders before the High Court.
Defence at departmental inquiry stage, review petitions, and writ petitions challenging dismissal, removal, and compulsory retirement orders passed without following due process.
Challenges to seniority lists, DPC decisions, zone of consideration, and promotion policies. Representation before the High Court on matters of statutory service rules and G.O.s.
Recovery of withheld pension, gratuity, and terminal benefits. Writ petitions for pensioners denied their entitlements under CCS Pension Rules and state government schemes.

08

FIR Quashing

His practice in this area extends across the Telangana High Court and the High Court of Andhra Pradesh, with a substantial volume of disposed petitions on record. Each matter requires a precise reading of the FIR, the material on record, and the applicable Supreme Court principles on maintainability before the High Court can be persuaded to exercise its inherent jurisdiction.

Matters Handled

Petitions for quashing of criminal proceedings under Section 498-A IPC (dowry harassment) before the Telangana and AP High Courts, typically following settlement between the parties.
Petitions where the FIR discloses no cognisable offence or where continuation of proceedings would be an abuse of process, based on the principles laid down by the Supreme Court.
Criminal revision petitions before the High Court challenging orders of Magistrate Courts and Sessions Courts, including interlocutory orders and conviction matters.
